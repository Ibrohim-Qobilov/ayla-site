Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Ayla ("we", "the extension") is a browser extension that replaces your browser's new tab page. It is developed by Ibrohim Qobilov (Uzbekistan), who is responsible for your data (the "controller"). This policy explains what data Ayla uses, where it is stored and what choices you have.

In short: Ayla works without an account and keeps your data in your browser. It shows no ads, has no analytics or tracking, and never sells your data.

1. Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage):

  • Your settings and main goal.
  • Tasks and task lists, notes, habits, reminders and your class schedule.
  • Focus statistics, achievements and the water log.
  • Favorite wallpapers, uploaded images, quick links (Speed Dial) and quotes.
  • The read-later list and saved tabs.
  • The prayer log and "Time on sites" data, if you use those features.

This data never leaves your device unless you sign in (section 2) or connect a service yourself (section 6). Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

2. Optional account and sync

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with email and password, Google or GitHub. Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we receive your email address, display name and profile photo URL from the provider you choose.

So that your data appears on all your devices, a copy of the following is stored in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Settings, main goal, tasks and task lists, notes, habits and reminders.
  • Your class schedule and water log.
  • Quotes, favorite wallpapers, quick links and the read-later list (page addresses and titles).
  • Focus statistics and achievements.

Each account can only read and write its own data. Nobody else, including other users, can access it.

Never synced:

  • Uploaded images and saved tabs.
  • "Time on sites" data.
  • The prayer log. Information about religious practice is a special category of data, so it stays on your device.
  • Your calendar connection.
  • Your Trello, Todoist and ClickUp keys.

3. Calendar (optional)

You can connect a calendar with an iCal link or with your Google account. With Google, Ayla requests read-only access (calendar.readonly).

  • Your events are used only to show the calendar widget.
  • Events are stored only on your device and are not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your calendars are never changed.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

Ayla's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is never used for advertising, never sold and never read by humans.

4. Location

Ayla uses your location to show weather, local currency and, where available, prayer times. It uses your browser's location (only if you allow it) or an approximate city based on your IP address. Your location is used only to request this information and is stored only on your device.

If you turn on rain and snow alerts, Ayla checks the forecast for your city once an hour in the background.

You can turn automatic location off and choose a city manually.

5. Features related to websites

All of these are optional. Their data stays on your device, except the read-later list, which is synced as described in section 2.

  • Site blocking and daily limits. Blocking works inside your browser, using only the sites you add to your list. If you set a daily limit, Ayla counts time only on those sites, and only while that tab is active.
  • Time on sites (Ayla Plus). This is off by default. If you turn it on and allow the "read your tabs" permission, Ayla records the site name (for example youtube.com, not the full address) of the active tab and how long it stays in front. It counts only while the browser window is in focus and you are at your computer. Ayla uses this data only to show you where your time went. It is kept on your device for 30 days, then deleted automatically. It is never synced, never added to backup files and never sent anywhere.
  • Saved tabs. When you click Save, Ayla stores the addresses and titles of the tabs in the current window.
  • Read later. When you use the right-click menu or the shortcut, Ayla saves the address and title of that page only.
  • Task from selected text. When you choose it from the right-click menu, the text you selected becomes a task.
  • Quick link suggestions. If you allow it, Ayla reads your most visited sites once, to suggest quick links. The list stays on your device.

Ayla does not collect your browsing history for any other purpose.

6. Integrations you connect yourself

  • Trello. You sign in on Trello's page (Atlassian). Ayla can then read your boards, show your account name, and create or update cards in the list you choose. The access key stays on your device. Tasks linked to cards sync together with your other tasks (section 2).
  • Todoist. You sign in on Todoist's page, or paste your personal API token. Ayla uses the access only to sync tasks between Ayla and Todoist (create, rename, change the date, complete). The keys stay on your device. When you sign in, Todoist's one-time code and, about once an hour while you use Ayla, the key renewal pass through our small Cloudflare server, which stores nothing.
  • ClickUp. You sign in on ClickUp's page. Ayla can then read your workspaces, lists and tasks, show the names and pictures of the people tasks are assigned to, and create or update tasks (status, name, due date, priority, assignees). If you choose lists to watch, Ayla checks them for new tasks every 5 minutes in the background; notifications about them are off unless you turn them on. The access key stays on your device. To finish signing in, ClickUp's one-time code passes through our small Cloudflare server, which exchanges it for the key and stores nothing. Tasks in the ClickUp tab sync together with your other tasks (section 2).

You can disconnect any of these services at any time in Settings.

8. Feedback when you uninstall

When you remove Ayla, a short survey page opens. Answering is optional and anonymous. If you answer, we receive only these:

  • the reason you pick;
  • your comment, if you write one;
  • the extension language and version.

The answer is stored in Firebase and also sent to the developer's private Telegram channel through a Cloudflare Worker, so we can read it quickly. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal data, so please don't write personal details in the comment.

9. Why we process your data (legal bases)

  • Account and sync: to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Location, calendar, Trello, Todoist, ClickUp and Time on sites: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by turning the feature off or disconnecting.

10. Where data is stored

Firestore data is stored in Google Cloud's European multi-region (Belgium and the Netherlands). Firebase Authentication is operated by Google and may process sign-in data in the United States, under Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

These countries are also on Uzbekistan's list of countries that ensure adequate protection of personal data.

11. How long we keep data

  • Account data: kept while your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: kept for 30 days so you can restore the account, then permanently deleted.
  • On your device: daily focus records are kept for 60 days, the prayer log for 90 days and Time on sites data for 30 days. Everything else stays until you delete it or uninstall Ayla.

12. Your rights

You can do all of the following at any time:

  • See and download all your data: Settings → Account → My data.
  • Correct your data.
  • Delete your account.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Object to processing.
  • Complain to your data protection authority.

For any request, contact us (section 16).

13. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads and don't use analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.
  • We don't use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • Ayla complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

14. Children

  • Accounts are for people aged 16 or older. People under 16 may create one only with a parent's or guardian's consent.
  • Without an account, anyone can use Ayla, and no data is collected.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the extension.
